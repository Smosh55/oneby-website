# "Looped" trademark screening

Date: July 29, 2026. Knockout-level search of live USPTO data (queried via
tmsearch.uspto.gov) plus market use. NOT a full attorney clearance.

## Findings

- No live federal registration of standalone "LOOPED" exists. Two pending
  applications: SKE Innovations (healthcare patient-engagement app, IC 9/42,
  currently SUSPENDED at the USPTO, which itself signals examiner-flagged
  LOOP-family conflicts) and a Class 45 social-networking filing from Jan 2026.
- The "LOOP" field is extremely crowded: 715 live marks containing "loop";
  159 in Class 9, 164 in Class 42, 23 in Class 38. Live exact-"LOOP" marks
  include MICROSOFT LOOP (registered, collaboration software), Benbria's LOOP
  (customer-feedback software, the closest to CRM), Loop Media (Class 38
  telecom/streaming), and HubSpot's pending LOOP MARKETING.
- Nearest commercial neighbor: Loop Communications (Charlotte NC, hosted
  business phone systems since 2009). Their federal filings are DEAD but they
  hold ~17 years of common-law rights in "Loop" for business telephony.
- The celebrity app "Looped Live" appears to operate on common-law rights
  only; no live federal filing surfaced.

## Assessment

- "Looped" for a keep-them-in-the-loop feature is suggestive-to-descriptive:
  a weak mark in a crowded field. Nobody can easily stop us; we cannot easily
  stop anyone.
- Standalone registration of "LOOPED" in Classes 9/42 is realistically
  unlikely (pending applications ahead in line; LOOP-family conflicts).
- House-branded "OneBy Looped" is materially lower risk and is the
  recommended posture: use it on the website, pricing, and anything external;
  bare "Looped" inside the product UI is fine because context supplies the
  OneBy association.

## Decision guidance

1. Use the name as "OneBy Looped" externally; "Looped" in-product. (The
   /looped page already carries the "Looped · OneBy" title pattern.)
2. Do not budget for standalone registration or enforcement; treat the name
   as a feature label, not a brand asset.
3. Watch items: SKE Innovations' pending LOOPED (IC 9/42), Benbria's LOOP,
   Loop Communications' common-law telephony rights.
4. If Looped becomes a flagship with real marketing spend, pay a trademark
   attorney for full clearance (~$500-1,500) before scaling the brand.
