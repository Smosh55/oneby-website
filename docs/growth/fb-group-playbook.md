# Facebook Group Playbook (first week + intro posts)

Companion to [targets.md](targets.md) (the group list) and
[outreach-kit.md](outreach-kit.md) (the value-post templates). Goal: get into the
owner groups, build standing, and let OneBy come up on its own, without tripping
the no-marketing rules almost all of these enforce.

Voice: owner to owner, plainspoken, no hype. No em dashes anywhere.

---

## The first-week cadence (per group)

- **Day 1, join and observe.** Join from your personal profile. Read the rules,
  and log the group's real size and promo policy in the tracker. Do not post.
- **Days 2 to 5, be useful.** Comment helpfully on 2 to 3 existing threads about
  pricing, take-home pay, callbacks, or the phone. No links, no product mention.
  You are just a sharp owner-adjacent person answering money questions.
- **Day 5 to 7, introduce (only where welcomed).** If the group has an intro
  thread or intros are normal, post Intro B below. If it is a strict no-promo
  room, skip the intro and post Question A instead.
- **Week 2 onward, one value post per week, max.** Rotate the three templates in
  outreach-kit.md section (c). Never post the same text in two groups in the same
  week; these groups cross-pollinate and repeat text reads as spam.

## The rules that keep you in

- One personal account, real name and photo. Never a brand page.
- Read each group's rules before your first post and log them. Roofing Insights
  and others ban marketing outright, one bad post and you are out.
- Link only where the rules allow it or when someone asks. The safest link
  anywhere is the free calculator (no email wall): oneby.ai/missed-call-calculator
- If a mod or member pushes back, thank them and stop. Never argue.
- Mark anyone who asks you to leave them alone as do-not-contact immediately.

---

## Post A, the question (safe in any group, no link)

Works even in strict no-promo rooms because it gives, it does not sell.

> Owners, honest question. When you are on a job with both hands full, who is
> answering your phone? Cell forwarded to voicemail, a spouse, an answering
> service, or does it just ring out?
>
> Trying to get a read on what most one to five truck shops actually do, because
> every option I can think of has a catch. What is working for you, and what
> drives you nuts about it?

Then answer every reply like a human. If someone asks what you are building, use
Reply C.

## Post B, the intro (only where intros are welcomed)

Honest disclosure, leads with contributing, no pitch, no link.

> Hey all, [YOUR NAME] here, [CITY]. I have spent the last stretch talking to
> trade owners about the after-hours phone problem, and I am building something
> in that space, so full disclosure, I have a horse in this race.
>
> Mostly I am here to learn and be useful on the office and phone side of the
> business. If anyone wants to swap notes on how you handle missed calls and
> callbacks, I am all ears.

## Reply C, when someone asks what you do

Natural, one link, zero pressure. Only after they ask.

> I am building OneBy, an AI that answers the calls you miss, gets the address
> and the problem, and leaves a ready-to-go job waiting for you in the morning.
> The software is free, you just pay for the phone line.
>
> Happy to send the demo if it is useful, no pressure either way. There is also a
> free calculator that shows what missed calls actually cost you, no email
> needed: oneby.ai/missed-call-calculator

---

## Which groups get the intro vs. the question

- **Intro B is fine in:** Blue Collar Millionaire, Home Service Expert, Grow Your
  Home Services Business, the HVAC/plumbing/electrical owner groups (most have
  intro culture). Still read the rules first.
- **Question A only (skip the intro) in:** Roofing Insights Private Group and any
  group whose rules explicitly ban marketing or self-promotion. Build standing
  purely by helping until someone asks.

See the tracker (fb-groups-tracker.csv) to record which is which as you go.
